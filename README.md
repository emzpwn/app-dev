# My Favorite Series

## The Summer I Turned Pretty

The Summer I Turned Pretty is a coming-of-age romantic drama about a teenage girl,
Belly Conklin, who spends her summers with her family at Cousins Beach.

### Main Characters
- Belly
- Conrad
- Jeremiah
- Steven
- Taylor
- Laurel
- Susannah

### Favorite Quote
> "It's hard to throw away history. It was like you were throwing away a part of yourself."🤝
